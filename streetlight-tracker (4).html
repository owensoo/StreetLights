This list is kept by neighbors, not by the City of East Cleveland. When you flip a switch or add a light, it's shared with everyone, usually within a few minutes. To get a light repaired, please also report it to The Illuminating Company (FirstEnergy) or the city.
Neighbors send this list to the City Service Department every week. City progress shows when it was sent, when the City confirmed receiving it, and any update from the City.
